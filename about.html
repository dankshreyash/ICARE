About ICARE

Overview

The Indian Centre for Academic Rankings & Excellence (ICARE) is devoted to quality advancement in Indian higher education.

The Indian Centre for Academic Rankings & Excellence (ICARE) is devoted to quality advancement in Indian higher education by fostering global competencies and ushering in a new revolution in the area of ‘World University Rankings’.

What Drives Us

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

The single most imperative goal ICARE aims to achieve is ‘to witness the entry of at least 5 of our elite universities in the Top 200 World University Rankings by 2025’.

Our Mission

To be the most trusted independent source of intelligence in the Indian Higher Education sector, delivering detailed analysis by being committed to the key values of rigorous integrity, undeniable value and accessible presentation.

Leadership & Research

The ICARE Advisory Board

The Advisory Board engages in research and architects the conception of tools that empower academicians, university leaders and governments.

  • Provides a comparative basis and high-quality global intelligence to help promote higher education standards.
  • Encourages long-term strategies for social and economic development in the Indian Higher Education sector.
  • Encourages self-evaluation, accountability, autonomy and innovation across institutions.
  • Provides access to extensive global data and corresponding education reports to universities.
  • Facilitates networking through groups and events held both online and in live environments.
  • Continuously identifies challenges and trends facing Indian Higher Education through research and development.
A Defining Milestone

The ‘Indian Universities Ranking’ Project

Based on interactions with Honorable President Shri Pranab Mukherjee and Honorable Prime Minister Shri Narendra Modi on May 12, 2014 and June 17, 2014 respectively, ICARE spearheaded the ‘Indian Universities Ranking’ project — an initiative to put together the ‘Top 100 Universities of India’.
