About ICARE

Quality Advancement in Indian Higher Education

The Indian Centre for Academic Rankings & Excellence (ICARE) is devoted to quality advancement in Indian higher education by fostering global competencies and ushering in a new revolution in the area of World University Rankings.

🎯

Our Vision

To witness the entry of at least 5 elite Indian universities in the Top 200 World University Rankings by 2025.

🚀

Our Mission

To be the most trusted independent source of intelligence in Indian Higher Education, committed to rigorous integrity, undeniable value and accessible presentation.

🏛️

Advisory Board

Research-driven tools that empower academicians, university leaders and governments with comparative global intelligence.
